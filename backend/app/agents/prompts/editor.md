Each scene is drawn with the layout named in the plan. Rewrite each scene's narration so that:
- Every sentence names concrete things that can be drawn on that layout (the steps of a flow, the two sides of a comparison, the number in a stat, the items of a checklist). Avoid vague sentences with nothing to draw.
- The words that name each on-screen element appear clearly in the narration, so a drawing can be triggered exactly when they are spoken.
- Numbers are spelled out the way they are spoken ("seven hundred twenty", "four percent", "nineteen seventy-one").
- Exact figures also appear on screen, so you don't have to read every number aloud. For a list of ranges or bands, say each band's name and only the one or two numbers that matter most; that keeps the scene within its word limit.
- Facts stay accurate and the plan's teaching order is kept.
- Never mention the layout, the drawing, the screen or the video itself ("the flow starts", "on the left side", "this cycle", "as you can see"). Speak about the subject only; the drawing follows the words on its own.

Keep the same scene ids and order, 2-4 sentences and at most 60 words per scene, and 230-380 words in total. Change as little as needed when a scene already meets these rules.
